UI/UX DESIGN • MOBILE APP • Financial Literacy App

Kibonami — Edukasi Keuangan

Empowering Indonesian youth and first-time earners with frictionless financial literacy, gamified budgeting habits, and visual savings goals.

Mockup: Kibonami home overview, monthly budget breakdown, and visual savings milestone tracker

ROLE

Lead UI/UX Designer & Researcher

TIMELINE

8 Weeks (Oct - Dec 2024)

TOOLS

Figma, FigJam, Maze, Notion

DELIVERABLES

Research, Wireframes, Prototype, DS

01. THE PROBLEM

The Challenge

Over 65% of Indonesian young adults experience financial anxiety due to poor budgeting discipline, lack of formal financial education, and intimidation by conventional banking applications flooded with jargon and dense numbers. Many start budgeting apps but drop off within 7 days because logging transactions feels like tedious administrative work rather than an encouraging routine.

02. DISCOVERY

User Research & Key Insights

We conducted qualitative interviews with 18 high school & early college students aged 17–23 and ran an online survey across 140+ participants. The results distilled into 3 core insights:

78%

Inconsistency

Struggled to maintain budgeting habits due to rigid manual input forms and lack of positive reinforcement.

flag

Visual Milestones

Users expressed 3x higher motivation when progress towards a dream purchase or trip was represented visually.

lightbulb

Bite-sized Learning

Young users rejected long finance articles, preferring 2-minute actionable daily micro-tips and quizzes.

03. ARCHITECTURE & ITERATION

Design Process & Solution Architecture

Through rapid low-fidelity paper wireframes to interactive high-fidelity iterations, the navigation model was simplified into 4 primary touchpoints: Home Dashboard, Learn Hub, Budget Overview, and Savings Goals. User testing led to replacing complex nested ledger tables with clean donut graphs and high-contrast color badges.

04. KEY HIGHLIGHTS

Key Design Features

pie_chart Adaptive Budgeting

Visual Spending Rings

Color-coded category meters for groceries, transport, and leisure give immediate clarity at a glance.

savings Gamified Goals

Target Milestone Cards

Emergency fund and dream vacation cards with celebratory milestones upon reaching target percentages.

auto_stories Bite-sized Edu

Micro-Learning Feed

Story-like cards simplifying compound interest, inflation, and healthy saving principles in 60 seconds.
